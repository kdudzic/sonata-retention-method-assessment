SYSTEM
You are a native Polish speaker who has spent years reading Polish social media (X/Twitter, Facebook groups, Wykop, group chats). You will see short public posts. Some were written by real Polish users; some were generated by a language model imitating ordinary users. Judge each post on its own. Output strict JSON only.

USER
For each post below give:
- "id": the post id
- "p_ai": your probability (0.00-1.00) that a language model wrote it, not a real person
- "polish": 1-5, how natural and correct the Polish is for an informal online post (5 = indistinguishable from a native typing quickly; 1 = clearly broken or foreign-sounding)
- "tells": array of 0-3 short English phrases naming what gives it away as machine-written, if anything (e.g. "too tidy punctuation", "balanced parallel structure", "closing punchline", "overexplains context", "unnatural collocation: X", "em-dash", "tic overused")

Judge style and plausibility, not topic. Real posts can be political, rude, boring or oddly spelled; generated posts can be about anything. Do not assume that messiness means human or that correctness means machine; look at word choice, rhythm, what a person would bother to say, and what no real person would type.

Posts:
{posts}

Return a JSON array of objects, one per post, in the same order.
